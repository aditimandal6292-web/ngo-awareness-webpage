About the NGO

InAmigos Foundation is a non-profit organization founded in 2020. The NGO works towards education, women empowerment, environmental sustainability, animal welfare, and community development. Their mission is to create a compassionate and empowered society through impactful social initiatives.

Ongoing Projects

Project SEVA

Providing food, clothes, and support to underprivileged communities.

Bachpanshala

Helping children get access to education and digital learning.

Project Udaan

Empowering women through skill development and financial independence.

Project Jeev

Animal welfare initiative focused on feeding and protecting stray animals.
